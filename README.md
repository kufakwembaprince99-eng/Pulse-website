THE STORY OF PULSE

PULSE was founded in 2024 by its owner and creative mind, KufakwembaPrince, with a vision to create a fashion label built around individuality, confidence and self-expression.

The name PULSE represents the rhythm of life — the energy that keeps us moving, creating and chasing our dreams.

From the beginning, KufakwembaPrince wanted PULSE to be more than just a clothing label. It was created as a statement: to stand out, embrace your identity and move to your own rhythm.

Blending luxury-inspired design with streetwear culture, PULSE continues to develop its own identity through bold details, distinctive designs and a commitment to originality.

What started as a vision in 2024 is becoming a movement.

PULSE — Feel the Pulse.
